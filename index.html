Featured product

GuzelBoard

Meet GuzelBoard. Your screens have more to say.

GuzelBoard transforms compatible TVs and streaming devices into powerful, easy-to-manage digital signs. Share announcements, menus, schedules, images, videos, websites, and Google Slides presentations — all managed remotely from one simple dashboard.

  • Simple to manage

    Upload content and manage displays remotely through a web dashboard.

  • Affordable to get started

    Use compatible Fire TV, Google TV, and Android TV devices — or Android tablets — without investing in specialized digital signage hardware.

  • Flexible content

    Show images, MP4 videos, websites, Google Slides, announcements, and scrolling text.

  • Smart scheduling

    Organize playlists and schedule when content appears.

  • Reliable playback

    Supported content can continue playing from the local cache during an internet interruption.
